Open an invite link to start syncing, or paste the address of your Now or Later Worker and its access token. How sync works. Leave both empty to keep your lists on this device only.

Your devices

The link connects another phone, tablet or computer to this board. Anyone who has it can open your lists, so send it only to yourself or to someone you share the board with.

Invite people

An invite link gives someone their own board on this Worker. It works once and expires after 14 days. The note is only for you, to remember who it's for.

    Delete your board

    This permanently deletes your board, its nightly copies and its access token from the sync service. Your other devices stop syncing. It can't be undone.

    On this device

    Save a copy of all your lists, including Done, to this device. Keep the backup file to restore it later. The CSV file opens in Numbers or Excel.

    Nightly copies

    The sync Worker saves a copy of your board every night and keeps it for 30 days.
